Overview

Context

Kinetic displays exist at the intersection of design, engineering, and interaction. The project explored how a physical object can communicate motion, rhythm, and information in a way that feels tangible rather than simply digital.

Challenge

The problem

The challenge was to turn a static visual idea into a living prototype that could demonstrate movement, intentional motion, and user engagement in a coherent system.

Project Snapshot

  • Domain: Interactive design
  • Focus: Motion-driven storytelling
  • Impact: Concept prototype
Journey

What was built

The concept evolved through iterative experimentation, balancing mechanical movement with design clarity. The result was a system that communicates presence and purpose through motion, not just static form.

Architecture

System thinking

Motion design

Movement patterns were chosen to make the display dynamic, legible, and memorable.

Interaction

Human response and perception shaped how the design communicates signals and intent.

Impact

Results and lessons

This project reinforced that physical interfaces can be powerful storytelling tools when form, movement, and intent are designed together. It was a study in how interaction becomes richer when it is felt, not just observed.
