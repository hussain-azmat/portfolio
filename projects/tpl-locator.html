01 · Overview

Context

TPL Locator is a BLE-based tracking solution for personal and asset visibility in environments where conventional SIM and GPS systems are limited or unavailable. The system is designed to work without relying on mobile connectivity or satellite positioning for core location estimation, instead using crowd-sourced intelligence and device-level telemetry.

That design choice runs against the default assumption in most tracking products — that a SIM card or a GPS chip is the starting point — and instead treats connectivity-free operation as the requirement the rest of the system has to be built around.

02 · Challenge

02Years of battery, without SIM or GPS

The key challenge was delivering a practical inventory and monitoring platform that could run for years on battery life while remaining accurate enough for personal and asset tracking.

The architecture needed to avoid dependence on SIM or GPS, support low-power operation, and still provide meaningful location visibility in real deployment conditions — three constraints that tend to pull against each other in most tracking hardware.

Project snapshot. Built for smart tracking and telematics · focused on BLE-based asset and personal monitoring · aimed at a long-life, low-cost tracking system.
03 · Journey

03From device to full telematics platform

The project includes device development, fleet and account logic, event monitoring, and a full operational dashboard. Over time, the focus expanded beyond just hardware tracking to include the full telematics experience: user management, geofencing, historical replay, asset monitoring, and real-time operational controls.

That expansion reflects how the platform grew from a single tracking problem into an operational tool — the device layer solves location, but the dashboard layer is what turns that location data into something a fleet or facility team can actually run on day to day.

04 · Architecture

04Device and platform

Hardware

BLE-enabled tracking devices engineered for low-power operation, long battery life, and practical field deployment without relying on GPS or mobile connectivity.

Software

Telematics data processing, user and asset management, event logic, geofencing, and dashboard workflows designed for operational visibility and analysis.

05 · Impact

Results and lessons

The solution demonstrates how telematics can be built around practical constraints rather than standard assumptions. Instead of depending on SIM or GPS, the platform uses a more resilient and cost-aware model to support personal and asset tracking.

The work also includes the broader system layer required for a real operational platform: dashboards, replay, boundaries, alerts, and access control — the parts of a tracking product that don't show up in a spec sheet but determine whether teams actually use it.
