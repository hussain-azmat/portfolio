Indoor Tracking Platform

Indoor Asset Tracking

A real-time indoor tracking system designed to improve visibility, operational control, and asset utilization in dynamic environments where conventional GPS-based methods are unreliable.

Overview

Context

Indoor environments often create a blind spot for asset visibility. Warehouses, facilities, campuses, and operational workspaces require precise monitoring of equipment, people, or mobile assets, but GPS and conventional location services become unreliable indoors.

Challenge

The problem

The core challenge was building a tracking architecture that could deliver consistent location visibility, process telemetry reliably, and provide useful operational insights without depending on external satellite infrastructure. This involved balancing cost, system uptime, accuracy, and maintainability.

Project Snapshot

  • Industry: Smart facilities / logistics
  • Focus: Indoor positioning and monitoring
  • Impact: Operational visibility and efficiency
Journey

What was built

The project focused on designing a system that could monitor asset movement in real-time while still remaining scalable and practical for deployment. The work spanned identifying the right sensing strategy, building the interfacing layer, and designing the reporting and dashboard experience so non-technical stakeholders could use the output effectively.

Architecture

System thinking

Hardware

Sensor devices, tracking nodes, and connectivity components were selected to fit the constraints of indoor deployment and real-world operating environments.

Software

The software architecture focused on telemetry collection, data processing, and a clean dashboard experience for monitoring assets and operational status.

Impact

Results and lessons

The platform demonstrated how practical, data-driven monitoring can transform day-to-day workflows, reduce blind spots, and improve operational decision making. More than the technical implementation, the project highlighted the importance of designing systems that align with real operational needs and are usable by the teams relying on them every day.
